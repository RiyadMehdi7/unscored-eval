Exact arithmetic & what this verdict means

Scores are bounded by 0 and 1; higher is better. Missing, error and unscored cells remain in the intended denominator. A reported error is unknown unless the input explicitly scores it as zero.

Only a strictly positive or strictly negative margin proves a winner. Endpoint ties are inconclusive; a margin fixed at zero is an exact tie. Displayed range percentages round outward, while exact fractions carry the proof. Known points and diagnostic means round to the nearest tenth of a percentage point.

This is a deterministic bound for this finite suite, not a statistical confidence interval or a prediction for unseen cases. The verdict depends on the supplied case set, weights and scoring policy.
